How to Play

Basic Care

Your plant needs three things: Water 💧, Sunlight ☀️, and Love ❤️. Use the buttons at the bottom to toggle rain and sun. Tap the pot to give love. Hold the pot to see your plant's vitality rings.

Growth & Evolution

Keep all three stats balanced for optimal growth. Your plant evolves through six stages: Shoot → Seedling → Sprout → Budding → Bloom → Flourish. At Bloom stage or higher, you can ascend to start a new generation.

Fireflies

When your plant is happy, fireflies may visit. Tap them to collect! Each of the 16 families has unique powers. Collect 50 of one family to unlock their Guardian.

Scars & Healing

Neglect can leave scars on your plant. If you see scars in the Care tab, tap them to begin a healing ritual and restore your plant.

Days of the Week

Each day brings special bonuses! Sun Day boosts sunlight. Moon Day enhances moonlight and Pearl fireflies. Tuesday offers growth rituals. Wednesday retains water and shares wisdom. Thursday increases thirst slightly. Friday boosts fireflies and rewards pot customization. Saturday slows decay and brings a helpful snail.

Trait Glossary
Each generation can pass down a trait to its offspring. These inherited traits shape your plant's growth and resilience.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 31, 2026

Welcome to Pocket Sprout. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information We Collect

For basic gameplay, we do not collect, store, or transmit personal information. However, to enable the Community Garden feature, we utilize Google Firebase to assign a random, anonymous identifier (ID) to your installation.

What the ID is for: It is used strictly to associate your in-game actions with the specific plant you are caring for. It is not linked to your personal identity.

Data Storage

Local game data is stored on your device. Community Garden data is stored via Google Firebase to enable real-time interaction.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for backend services. We use GitHub Pages to serve static content to you. We do not use advertising or analytics platforms.
