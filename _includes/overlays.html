📖 How to Play

🌱 Basic Care

Water (💧): Toggle rain to keep soil moist. Too much drowns!

Sunlight (☀️): Toggle lamp for light. Evaporates water faster.

Love (❤️): Tap the pot to show affection.

Vitality Rings: Tap and hold the pot to see your plant's water, sun, and love levels as colored rings.

🌿 Boosts

Sing (🎵): Gives a burst of growth. 5 minute cooldown.

Fertilize (🌿): Doubles growth speed for 60 seconds. 3 minute cooldown.

🦋 Fireflies

Tap glowing fireflies to collect them. Each family has unique powers.

Collect 10 of one family to unlock their Guardian — a larger firefly that appears in the sky and provides passive bonuses when tapped!

🧬 Inheritance

When your plant ascends at full bloom, its child inherits a trait. Each generation shapes the next.

🩹 Neglect & Scars

Letting vitality drop below 20% causes stress. Extended neglect leaves permanent scars. Severe crisis leads to withering.

Watch your rings! Pulsing red means your plant needs urgent care.
