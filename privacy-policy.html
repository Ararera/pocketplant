Introduction

Welcome to Pocket Sprout. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information We Collect

Pocket Sprout is designed with your privacy in mind. For basic gameplay, we do not collect, store, or transmit personal information. However, to enable the Community Garden feature, we utilize Google Firebase to assign a random, anonymous identifier (ID) to your installation.

What the ID is for: It is used strictly to associate your in-game actions with the specific plant you are caring for. It is not linked to your personal identity.

Data Storage

Local game data is stored locally on your device. Community Garden data is stored via Google Firebase to enable real-time interaction.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for backend services. We use GitHub Pages to serve static content to you. We do not use advertising or analytics platforms.

Children's Privacy

Pocket Sprout does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated. Changes will appear on this page.
