Privacy Policy for Pocket Sprout

Last updated: January 30, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Pocket Sprout. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

Information We Collect

Pocket Sprout is designed with your privacy in mind. For basic gameplay, we do not collect, store, or transmit personal information. However, to enable the Community Garden feature, we utilize Google Firebase to assign a random, anonymous identifier (ID) to your installation.

This ID is used strictly to associate your in-game actions with the specific plant you are caring for, ensuring smooth gameplay operations and state consistency within the community features. It is not linked to your personal identity, email, or physical location.

Data Storage

Local game data (such as offline progress and collected items) is stored locally on your device using standard web storage technologies. Data related to the Community Garden feature (including your random ID and plant status) is stored in the cloud via Google Firebase to facilitate real-time interactions.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase to provide backend infrastructure for the Community Garden. Firebase may collect certain data, such as IP addresses and instance IDs, to maintain service security and reliability. We do not use any other third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Pocket Sprout does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The random ID used for the Community Garden is anonymous and does not allow us to identify specific users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our GitHub repository at github.com/ararera.
