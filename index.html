📖 How to Play

🌱 Basic Care

Water (💧): Toggle rain to keep soil moist. Too much drowns!

Sunlight (☀️): Toggle lamp for light. Evaporates water faster.

Love (❤️): Tap the pot to show affection.

🌿 Boosts

Sing (🎵): Use the 'Sing' action to give a burst of growth.

Fertilize (🌿): Apply fertilizer to temporarily double growth speed.

🌅 Time of Day

The world syncs with real time. Day provides sun, Night provides soft moonlight (unless it's a new moon!).

🦋 Fireflies

Tap fireflies to collect them. Each family has unique powers. Collect 10 of one family to summon a Guardian!

🧬 Inheritance

When your plant ascends, its child inherits traits. Each generation shapes the next.

🩹 Scars

Neglect causes scars (wilting, bending). Scars remain for the plant's life. Severe neglect leads to death.
